\section{Profile}
\cvitem{}{Mathematician with a PhD in algebraic geometry from the Universitat Aut\`onoma de
Barcelona (2020) and five peer-reviewed publications. My work has ranged from pure algebraic
geometry to reaction-network theory and numerical algebraic geometry. I have taught
mathematics and statistics at university level for eight years, and I build software: Julia
packages with tests, continuous integration and documentation, and an Android app. Based in
Barcelona.}
