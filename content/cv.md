Mathematician with a PhD in algebraic geometry from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona
(2020) and five peer-reviewed publications. My work has ranged from pure algebraic geometry
to reaction-network theory and numerical algebraic geometry. I have taught mathematics and
statistics at university level for eight years, and I build software: Julia packages with
tests, continuous integration and documentation, and an Android app. Based in Barcelona.

### Research

**Postdoctoral Researcher**, Department of Mathematical Sciences, University of Copenhagen —
January 2020 to December 2021.
In the Mathematical Biology group, mentored by Elisenda Feliu. Real algebraic geometry
methods for parametric reaction networks, funded by the Novo Nordisk Foundation. I
implemented algorithms for the steady-state analysis of biologically motivated dynamical
systems; the work produced the paper *Disguised toric dynamical systems*.

## Software

- **SymbolicCRN.jl** — author and maintainer. A registered Julia package for symbolic analysis
  of chemical reaction networks, parametrising steady states by convex parameters; it bridges
  Catalyst.jl and OSCAR. Continuous integration on GitHub Actions, tests, coverage,
  documentation on GitHub Pages, automated tagging and releases, MIT licensed.
- **BudanTables.jl** — Julia package for plotting Budan's tables of polynomials, with
  pluggable root-finding backends.
- **PKPDModels.jl** — Julia package for pharmacometric modelling.
- **SoftReminder** — an Android application in Kotlin, designed and built independently. See
  [SoftReminder](/softreminder).
- Scripts and notebooks supporting my publications: Macaulay2, OSCAR, Singular and polymake.
- Tooling and configuration: a Doom Emacs configuration, LaTeX templates for collaborative
  mathematical writing, and Org-mode workflows.
