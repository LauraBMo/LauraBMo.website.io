Sessions for [Bojos per les matemàtiques](https://www.fundaciocatalunya-lapedrera.com/),
a programme that brings secondary-school students into research mathematics. The session
material is in Catalan.

## Bits, *SCM/Notícies*

Short pieces for the *Bits* section of *SCM/Notícies*, the magazine of the Societat Catalana
de Matemàtiques, all written in Catalan — with Martí Prats Soler, and later with Odí Soler i
Gibert. Each takes one tool or one corner of mathematics and shows it working.

- *Surfejant superfícies* — no. 46, 2020. Drawing algebraic surfaces by typing their
  equations, and playing with them.
- *Un tastet de topologia algebraica* — no. 47, 2020. Polyhedra, their combinatorics, and
  the polymake software.
- *LaTeX per a no iniciats* — no. 48, 2021. What LaTeX is, why a mathematician would bother,
  and how to start.
- *Divulgació matemàtica en línia* — no. 49, 2021. Online popular mathematics — channels,
  podcasts and sites worth following.
- *Mani'm?* — no. 50, 2022. Manim, the Python engine behind 3Blue1Brown's animations, and
  making a first video with it.
- *Jocs amb matemàtiques* — no. 51, 2023. Mathematics in mobile games, from logic puzzles to
  simple equation solving.
- *Programant amb Julia* — no. 52, 2023. The Julia language, and where it differs from
  Python, C and R.
