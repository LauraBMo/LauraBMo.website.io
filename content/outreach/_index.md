## Bits, *SCM/Notícies*

Short pieces for the *Bits* section of *SCM/Notícies*, the magazine of the Societat Catalana
de Matemàtiques, all written in Catalan — with Martí Prats Soler, and later with Odí Soler i
Gibert. Each takes one tool or one corner of mathematics and shows it working.

- *Surfejant superfícies* — no. 46, 2020 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/N46.pdf). Drawing algebraic surfaces by
  typing their equations, and playing with them.
- *Un tastet de topologia algebraica* — no. 47, 2020 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/N47.pdf). Polyhedra, their
  combinatorics, and the polymake software.
- *LaTeX per a no iniciats* — no. 48, 2021 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/N48.pdf). What LaTeX is, why a
  mathematician would bother, and how to start.
- *Divulgació matemàtica en línia* — no. 49, 2021 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/N49.pdf). Online popular mathematics —
  channels, podcasts and sites worth following.
- *Mani'm?* — no. 50, 2022 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/N50.pdf). Manim, the Python engine
  behind 3Blue1Brown's animations, and making a first video with it.
- *Jocs amb matemàtiques* — no. 51, 2023 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/SCMNoticies-51_WEB_ok.pdf). Mathematics
  in mobile games, from logic puzzles to simple equation solving.
- *Programant amb Julia* — no. 52, 2023 ·
  [PDF](https://scm.iec.cat/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Noticies-52_op-web.pdf). The Julia
  language, and where it differs from Python, C and R.
