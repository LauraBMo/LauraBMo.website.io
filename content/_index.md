# Laura Brustenga i Moncusí

{{< figure class="avatar" src="/avatar.jpg" alt="Laura Brustenga i Moncusí" >}}

I am a mathematician. My PhD, at the [Universitat Autònoma de
Barcelona](https://www.uab.cat/) under [Joaquim Roé](https://mat.uab.cat/~jroe/), was in
algebraic geometry. Afterwards I was a postdoc at the [Department of Mathematical
Sciences](https://www.math.ku.dk/english/) of the [University of
Copenhagen](https://www.ku.dk/english/), in the [Mathematical
Biology](https://www.math.ku.dk/english/research/spt/reaction-networks/) group mentored
by [Elisenda Feliu](http://web.math.ku.dk/~efeliu/), working on reaction networks.

Publications, preprints and talks are on the [research](/research) page; the fuller
record is in the [CV](/cv). I also build [SoftReminder](/softreminder).
